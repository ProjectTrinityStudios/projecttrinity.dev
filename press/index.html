About the game

Project Trinity: Shatterbound is a hub-based dungeon crawler built around the Holy Trinity of MMO raiding. Four heroes, Bastion the Tank, Oracle the Healer and Reaver and Asha on damage, fight through dungeons of trash packs and bosses with telegraphed mechanics.

The player can swap into any of them mid-fight while the AI party plays the rest. That party is made to be extremely competent, like a raid team chasing leaderboards and day-one clears, and it's never throttled; the only thing held back is knowledge, and only where that serves the raid leader fantasy.

The heroes

  • Bastion Tank The wall at the front. Every hit he blocks builds the Resolve he spends on Vengeful Riposte.
  • Oracle Healer Heals from range. Her Mana comes back only when Glimmer and Purifying Lance land, so she fights to keep healing.
  • Reaver Melee DPS A thief with two blades. Hits from behind earn more Momentum, and Grand Larceny and Swagger spend it.
  • Asha Ranged DPS Leaves potion zones on the ground and pops them with her pistols.
