Devlog · July 2026

July 2026: the first fight

Covers July 2026. Written in November 2026 from the project's records.

Project Trinity: Shatterbound started on July 13th, 2026, as an empty folder and one idea: a dungeon game built around the Holy Trinity of MMO raiding, a tank, a healer and damage, where the rest of your party plays beside you.

The next day the game could already fight. By July 14th it had its rules for damage, healing, threat (who the enemy is angry at), dodging, and the glowing floor warnings that show where an attack will land. The first three heroes arrived that same day: Bastion the tank, who blocks to build up his Resolve; Oracle the healer, who aims her spells; and Reaver, who hits hardest from behind.

On July 16th the party started thinking for itself. The tank, healer and damage each played their role on their own, and you could steer how carefully or how boldly they played. The same week brought the first boss: Maera, the Drowned Warden, the keeper of a dead lighthouse, fought in a flooded cave called the Lightless Shoal, with rising water, geysers and robed Tidecallers healing her from the back.

Late in the month came the idea the whole game is built on. From July 28th you could jump into any member of your party in the middle of a fight: one second you're tanking, the next you're the healer, with a short slow-down while you travel and a moment of safety when you land. On July 29th the game was built as a real Windows program for the first time, and it built clean on the first try.

And on July 30th and 31st, the party got its fourth member: Asha, a ranged fighter who throws potions out of a floating bag that never runs out. She was the first hero added after the first three, and she has been with the party ever since.
