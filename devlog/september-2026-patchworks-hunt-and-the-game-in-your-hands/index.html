September 2026: Patchwork's Hunt, and the game in your hands

Covers September 2026. Written in November 2026 from the project's records.

September was a month of building on the lighter way of working that began after the Great Reset. Its focus was one fight, made properly: Patchwork's Hunt.

Patchwork, the giant made of five parts, became a full boss fight in his own chamber. To beat him, the party has to dodge his hammer, answer his gun, interrupt his wizard's spell and read his shadow charge. On September 17th the main menu's Play button took you straight into his fight.

The heroes found out where they come from. On September 2nd each one got a home world: Asha from a cyberpunk city, Reaver from a steampunk one, and Bastion and Oracle from a world of warlocks. At the end of the month Bastion and Oracle began to wear a painted warlock look to match.

On September 5th the game ran on a handheld for the first time: a build of Patchwork's fight made for the ROG Xbox Ally X. From then on, the game had to play well in your hands, not just on a desk.

The fight got more of its feel. A box on screen shows when the boss is stunned (September 4th), a banner shows when a hero is downed (September 10th), and the boss gets his name across the top of the screen. On September 24th bodies started to react when they're hit: they flinch, and a downed hero turns grey. The heroes' attacks and skills were reworked the same day.

There was a lot to learn about the party AI, too. In one test on September 17th the tank was left out entirely, and the party survived Patchwork anyway: Reaver picked up the boss's attention and kept stepping out of his swings, while Oracle healed what got through.

By the end of September the fight was getting its weight: a new Skills page, a cast bar, the first skill icons for Reaver, and hit sounds chosen with one rule: how a hit feels comes first, starting with Reaver.
