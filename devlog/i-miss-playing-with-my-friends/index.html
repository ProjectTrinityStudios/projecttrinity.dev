Devlog · 25 November 2026

I miss playing with my friends and raiding. So I built this.

I built it because I miss playing with my friends. TERA shut down, everybody's busy and in different time zones now, and some raid buddies of mine have died, so I can never really play with them again. So I've built some AI party members to help me.

Project Trinity: Shatterbound is a hub-based dungeon crawler built around the Holy Trinity, like in MMORPGs: a tank, a healer and DPS. It takes some influence from TERA, WoW and Final Fantasy 14, the famous raiding games.

The party

Project Trinity is also the AI party, the tank, healer and DPS that run with you. The goal was to make them extremely competent, like my own days chasing leaderboards, number ones and day-one clears. They're never throttled. The only thing I take away is knowledge, and only where it helps with the raid leader fantasy and the dungeon raiding fantasy. I just tell it less.

Along with that, you can swap characters mid-fight: one second you're tanking, the next you're the healer.

Watch it

In the video, the party at the first boss is all AI, the tank included. The two-player parts are me and my girlfriend on one screen. Watch the gameplay on YouTube.

How it's made

I architected the project, guided it, and gave my opinions on how the AI should play, how the bosses should play and how everything should lay out. It's built in Unity, every dungeon and set piece is made in Blender, and the animations, music and sound effects are packs I bought.

What surprised me most: AI can't hear, and it can't really see, it only sees frames. So if you're going to build something this big, be prepared to listen to a lot of audio and watch a lot of recordings and clips. I sure know what wood sounds like and what a metal blade on flesh sounds like.

Where it's at

The game is in alpha right now. There are more plans: more characters, more dungeons, and online co-op is a goal.
