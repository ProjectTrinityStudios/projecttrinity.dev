Meet Project Trinity: Shatterbound

Project Trinity: Shatterbound is a hub-based dungeon crawler built around the Holy Trinity of MMO raiding: a tank, a healer and DPS. It takes its cues from the famous raiding games: TERA, World of Warcraft and Final Fantasy XIV.

The party

Project Trinity is also the AI party: the tank, healer and DPS that run with you. They're made to be extremely competent, like a raid team chasing leaderboards and day-one clears, and they're never throttled. The only thing held back is knowledge, and only where that serves the raid leader fantasy.

You can swap characters mid-fight: one second you're tanking, the next you're the healer.

The video

In the gameplay video, the party at the first boss is all AI, the tank included. The two-player parts are two people sharing one screen. Watch the gameplay on YouTube.

How it's made

Shatterbound is built in Unity, and every dungeon and set piece is made in Blender. The animations, music and sound effects are licensed packs. The party AI is the game's own research.

Where it's at

The game is in alpha, with builds for Windows, Mac and Linux. More characters and more dungeons are planned, and online co-op is a goal.
