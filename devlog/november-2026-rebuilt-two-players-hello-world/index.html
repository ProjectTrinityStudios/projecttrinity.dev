Devlog · November 2026

November 2026: rebuilt, two players, and hello world

Covers November 1st to 25th, 2026. Written in November 2026 from the project's records.

November began with a fresh start and ended with the game out in the open.

On November 1st the old version of the game was put away, safely kept, and the rebuild began, one for one in a fresh project, the plan called Path B. It was meant to take eight to twelve weeks. It took two. The Hungry Deep played in the new game by November 15th, and on November 16th Path B was finished. There was never a party for it.

Patchwork's Hunt came back too, along with Boss Rush: three quick runs straight to the Giant, Old Hunger and Maera. The Hunt and every Boss Rush were played through on November 12th.

Two players, one screen. On November 16th a second player could pick up a controller and join: the screen splits, both players can steer the AI party, and if you leave and come back, you take your hero back. On November 21st two players sat down on one screen, and what they found got fixed that same day: one boss health bar instead of two, menus you can move with the stick, and no shared slow-down when one player swaps.

Asha got a new look. After a sit-down on November 15th she got a whole new body, and on November 20th it was approved.

The game also learned to sound like itself. Reaver's footsteps, hits, death and dodge got sounds picked by ear (November 20th), Asha's attacks got their liquid-and-glass effects, and each door and lock got a sound for what it's made of.

On November 24th the game was built for Mac and Linux for the first time. A day later a friend ran the Linux version on their Legion Go handheld under SteamOS and wrote back that it ran great, about 100 frames a second in the first dungeon.

Then, on November 25th, Project Trinity: Shatterbound was shown publicly for the first time, with its first video on Reddit, and the same day projecttrinity.dev went live.

Coming: Don Fortuna, the boss first drawn in August. Her whole fight exists in the game's rules today, Engines, Card Table and all, but you can't play her yet. Fortuna is next.
