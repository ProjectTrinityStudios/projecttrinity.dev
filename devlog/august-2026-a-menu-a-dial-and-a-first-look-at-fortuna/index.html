Devlog · August 2026

August 2026: a menu, a dial and a first look at Fortuna

Covers August 2026. Written in November 2026 from the project's records.

August was the month the game started to feel like something you could sit down and play, and the month the project learned the most about how not to build it.

On August 2nd the game got its first main menu. The same day, the first concept art was drawn for Don Fortuna, who runs the High Table, a casino where the House keeps every draw loaded, with a Pit Boss at her side. She stood in the test arena by August 3rd. You can't fight her yet, but she has been waiting in the wings ever since.

The four heroes moved into one shared format in early August, so each hero is now described by its own file instead of being hand-built every time. That made the party easier to grow, and on August 8th Oracle and Reaver were checked and fought exactly the same way after the move.

The Trinity Dial, your way of telling the AI party how to play, got its own design and controls. By the end of the month it sat on screen as its own widget, with presets on single keys, ordered from safest to riskiest.

The fight got easier to read. A hero who falls is now downed, not gone, and can be rescued (August 14th). A row on screen shows who the boss is focused on. The floor warnings got their colours from one palette, and heals turned green. If every hero falls, the pull ends as a wipe, just like in a raid.

A new boss took shape: Patchwork, a giant made of five parts, each one teaching one thing to watch for: a hammer, a cyber arm, a stone wizard's channel, a shadow mark and a gun. He was designed on August 19th and had his first playable moves by August 25th.

Then, on August 31st, the project stopped and started over, not the game but the way it was run. The rules and notes around the project had grown so big that keeping up with them took more effort than making the game. So they were cleared out, an archive was kept, and September started with a much lighter way of working. That day is called the Great Reset.
