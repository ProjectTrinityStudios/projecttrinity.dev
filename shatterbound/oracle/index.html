The heroes / Healer

Oracle

Healer Resource: Mana

Heals from range. Her Mana comes back only when Glimmer and Purifying Lance land, so she fights to keep healing.

Oracle regains Mana only by landing Glimmer and Purifying Lance.

Play her yourself, or swap to another hero mid-fight and the party AI plays her for you.

Oracle's skills

  • Glimmer Primary Oracle fires bolts of light, regaining Mana with each hit. Three casts chain into one another.
  • Flicker Step Secondary Oracle teleports to the spot she is aiming at.
  • Mending Bolt Oracle heals the friend she targets with a bolt that follows them and passes through walls.
  • Circle of Renewal Oracle places a circle of light. Friends in it heal every second while it lasts.
  • Purifying Lance Oracle throws a lance of light that burns whatever it hits. A hit pours Mana back into her.
  • Aegis Chorus Oracle sings to the party. When the song ends, everyone heals and their Defense rises for a time.
  • Intervention Oracle charges a beam that heals friends and burns enemies in its path. The longer she charges, the farther it reaches; she cannot move while charging.

Healer in each fight

  • Patchwork When The Collector calls in a debt, the two heroes with Debt come first.
  • The Giant The Storm's burn stacks with every hit. Top people up as it ends.
  • Old Hunger Seize takes whoever stands farthest away, so stay with the party. Heal everyone up after each Retch.
  • Maera The Lamp is your second tank. Heal it every time it flickers.
