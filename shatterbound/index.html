The game

Shatterbound

Four heroes and bosses built like raid fights. Play alone, or with a friend on one split screen, and the party AI plays whichever heroes you don't. Swap into any hero mid-fight.

The heroes

Each hero holds down a scroll. Open it for their key skills, or go on to their own page.

Tank

  • Bastion Tank · Resolve Open his scrollRoll it up

    The wall at the front. Every hit he blocks builds the Resolve he spends on Vengeful Riposte.

    • Block Secondary Bastion raises his shield and blocks hits from the front until it tires. Raising it just as a hit lands makes his next damaging skill hit harder.
    • Challenger's Roar Bastion roars, dealing damage to every enemy around him and taunting them.
    • Clear Ground Bastion strikes every enemy around him with his shield's stored damage, and clears the lingering pools enemies have left on the ground near him.
    All of Bastion's skills, and his job in each fight

    • Circle of Renewal Oracle places a circle of light. Friends in it heal every second while it lasts.
    • Aegis Chorus Oracle sings to the party. When the song ends, everyone heals and their Defense rises for a time.

DPS

  • Reaver Melee DPS · Momentum Open his scrollRoll it up

    • Pickpocket Reaver dashes forward with a flurry of cuts, and they cut deeper right after Sleight of Hand.
    • Grand Larceny Reaver spins through every enemy around him, cutting again and again.

The bosses

In the order you meet them. Each fight is folded away until you open it.

Patchwork

Patchwork's Hunt

Since the crash, nobody goes past the broken gate. Something huge walks the wrecked halls in there, built of parts that do not belong together.

How the fight works (spoilers)
  • Cartoon Hammer: “I'll stamp you out!” A chain of slams on the party. Every slam that lands earns him another.
  • Cybernetic Tank Buster comes after two Cybernetic Stomps: a cone in front of him that can't be blocked. If it hits, Cybernetic Firearm follows on someone else.
  • Stone Wizard Channel: stun him and he staggers; let it finish and Meteor Shower falls.
  • Warlock Shadow: “Who stole my shadow?” He marks a hero and Shadow Charges at them. Charging into a Crash Pillar leaves him Vulnerable: he can't attack and takes more damage.
  • The Collector puts Debt, a growing damage over time, on two heroes.

Your job

Tank

Step out of the Tank Buster's cone; your shield won't stop it.

Healer

When The Collector calls in a debt, the two heroes with Debt come first.

DPS

Lead his Shadow Charge into a Crash Pillar, then hit him hard while he's Vulnerable.

Patchwork's own page

The Giant

The Hungry Deep: The Giant's Gate

A Giant keeps the gate to the Hungry Deep. Whatever the Chanters feed down there, nothing reaches it without getting past him first.

How the fight works (spoilers)
  • Swipe, and Swipe into Cleave: wide arcs in front of him with no warning on the floor. Cleave is the heavy one.
  • Storm, at half health and again near the end: a ring around him that hits three times and can't be blocked. Every hit leaves a burn that stacks.
  • No extra enemies and no phases: it's you and him.

Your job

Tank

Keep him facing you. Swipe and Cleave give no warning.

Healer

The Storm's burn stacks with every hit. Top people up as it ends.

DPS

Get out of the Storm's ring early: every hit you take adds to the burn.

The Giant's own page

Old Hunger

The Hungry Deep: The Feeding Pit

Something is eating its way up from below the Crossroads. The Chanters call it Old Hunger and keep it fed. It is small for now, and they mean to change that.

How the fight works (spoilers)
  • Imps crawl out of the Tears and feed him. Every Fed stack makes him hit harder. When “HE'S FULL”, he Retch Slams around himself and a Retch Shower hunts the party.
  • The Devouring Glutton comes through a Tear and casts Seize on whoever stands farthest away, never the Tank: “THE GLUTTON HAS ONE OF US”. That hero is Held and can't move, cast, dodge or block. The party calls it mind control.
  • The Glutton drags its catch to Old Hunger. If it reaches him, he takes them both and hits far harder. Kill the Glutton first and the hero goes free.
  • Bile Spit leaves pools of bile that stay, and every pool makes him stronger (Pooled Bile). Bastion's Clear Ground clears them.
  • Burrow: he tunnels to the farthest hero and comes up with Erupt. Up close: Snap Bite and Head Rake in front, a Tail Sweep behind.

Your job

Tank

Hold him away from the bile. As Bastion, Clear Ground wipes the pools.

Healer

Seize takes whoever stands farthest away, so stay with the party. Heal everyone up after each Retch.

DPS

Kill Imps before they reach him. On “THE GLUTTON HAS ONE OF US”, switch to the Glutton and kill it.

Old Hunger's own page

Maera

The Hungry Deep: The Lightless Shoal

The lamp on the Lightless Shoal has gone dark. Its keeper, Maera, still walks the shore, but something else looks out through her eyes.

How the fight works (spoilers)
  • The Lamp is on your side. If it goes out, the fight is lost. It starts low and calls for healing: “THE LAMP CALLS FOR HEALING”.
  • Spume Crawlers, Catfish and Rakes come for the Lamp. A Spume Crawler killed near it leaves Lamplight, a pool that heals it.
  • The Dark: your damage to her is cut to a tenth.
  • The Dark Ring follows her. Heroes in The Small Light are safe from it, and while she stands in it her Deep Hunger can't grow.
  • When she Sinks, dodge Lantern Rain, The Dark Wheel, Drowning Chains and The Chasing Tide. Between those: Anchor Slam, which can't be blocked, and Anchor Throw, which hooks a hero in for Anchor Spin.

Your job

Tank

Hold her inside The Small Light as it moves.

Healer

The Lamp is your second tank. Heal it every time it flickers.

DPS

Kill the enemies going for the Lamp, and kill Spume Crawlers close to it.

Maera's own page

Don Fortuna Coming

The High Table

Don Fortuna runs the High Table, a casino where the House rigs every draw while its engines stand. Break an engine, and that draw plays fair.
