The bosses / The Hungry Deep: The Lightless Shoal

Maera

The lamp on the Lightless Shoal has gone dark. Its keeper, Maera, still walks the shore, but something else looks out through her eyes.

How the fight works

Open the walkthrough (spoilers)
  • The Lamp is on your side. If it goes out, the fight is lost. It starts low and calls for healing: “THE LAMP CALLS FOR HEALING”.
  • Spume Crawlers, Catfish and Rakes come for the Lamp. A Spume Crawler killed near it leaves Lamplight, a pool that heals it.
  • The Dark: your damage to her is cut to a tenth.
  • The Dark Ring follows her. Heroes in The Small Light are safe from it, and while she stands in it her Deep Hunger can't grow.
  • When she Sinks, dodge Lantern Rain, The Dark Wheel, Drowning Chains and The Chasing Tide. Between those: Anchor Slam, which can't be blocked, and Anchor Throw, which hooks a hero in for Anchor Spin.

Your job

Tank

Hold her inside The Small Light as it moves.

Healer

The Lamp is your second tank. Heal it every time it flickers.

DPS

Kill the enemies going for the Lamp, and kill Spume Crawlers close to it.
