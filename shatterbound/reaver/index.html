The heroes / Melee DPS

Reaver

Melee DPS Resource: Momentum

A thief with two blades. Hits from behind earn more Momentum, and Grand Larceny and Swagger spend it.

Reaver earns Momentum with every hit, and more from behind. Grand Larceny and Swagger spend it.

Play him yourself, or swap to another hero mid-fight and the party AI plays him for you.

Reaver's skills

  • Quick Hands Primary Reaver strikes the enemies in front of him. Hits from behind earn more Momentum. Three strikes chain into one another.
  • Ankle Tax Secondary Reaver slashes at the target's legs again and again, slowing it for a moment.
  • Sleight of Hand Reaver distracts the enemies in front of him with quick slashes, leaving them open to Pickpocket.
  • Pickpocket Reaver dashes forward with a flurry of cuts, and they cut deeper right after Sleight of Hand.
  • Clean Sweep Reaver swings his blades, sending cutting waves out in front of him.
  • Grand Larceny Reaver spins through every enemy around him, cutting again and again.
  • Swagger Reaver struts for a while, moving faster with more crit chance.

DPS in each fight

  • Patchwork Lead his Shadow Charge into a Crash Pillar, then hit him hard while he's Vulnerable.
  • The Giant Get out of the Storm's ring early: every hit you take adds to the burn.
  • Old Hunger Kill Imps before they reach him. On “THE GLUTTON HAS ONE OF US”, switch to the Glutton and kill it.
  • Maera Kill the enemies going for the Lamp, and kill Spume Crawlers close to it.
