The bosses / The Hungry Deep: The Giant's Gate

The Giant

A Giant keeps the gate to the Hungry Deep. Whatever the Chanters feed down there, nothing reaches it without getting past him first.

How the fight works

Open the walkthrough (spoilers)
  • Swipe, and Swipe into Cleave: wide arcs in front of him with no warning on the floor. Cleave is the heavy one.
  • Storm, at half health and again near the end: a ring around him that hits three times and can't be blocked. Every hit leaves a burn that stacks.
  • No adds and no phases: it's you and him.

Your job

Tank

Keep him facing you. Swipe and Cleave give no warning.

Healer

The Storm's burn stacks with every hit. Top people up as it ends.

DPS

Get out of the Storm's ring early: every hit you take adds to the burn.
