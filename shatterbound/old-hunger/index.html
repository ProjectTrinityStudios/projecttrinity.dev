The bosses / The Hungry Deep: The Feeding Pit

Old Hunger

Something is eating its way up from below the Crossroads. The Chanters call it Old Hunger and keep it fed. It is small for now, and they mean to change that.

How the fight works

Spoilers from here on.

  • Imps crawl out of the Tears and feed him. Every Fed stack makes him hit harder. When “HE'S FULL”, he Retch Slams around himself and a Retch Shower hunts the party.
  • The Devouring Glutton comes through a Tear and casts Seize on whoever stands farthest away, never the Tank: “THE GLUTTON HAS ONE OF US”. That hero is Held and can't move, cast, dodge or block. The party calls it mind control.
  • The Glutton drags its catch to Old Hunger. If it reaches him, he takes them both and hits far harder. Kill the Glutton first and the hero goes free.
  • Bile Spit leaves pools of bile that stay, and every pool makes him stronger (Pooled Bile). Bastion's Clear Ground clears them.
  • Burrow: he tunnels to the farthest hero and comes up with Erupt. Up close: Snap Bite and Head Rake in front, a Tail Sweep behind.

Your job

Tank

Hold him away from the bile. As Bastion, Clear Ground wipes the pools.

Healer

Seize takes whoever stands farthest away, so stay with the party. Heal everyone up after each Retch.

DPS

Kill Imps before they reach him. On “THE GLUTTON HAS ONE OF US”, switch to the Glutton and kill it.
