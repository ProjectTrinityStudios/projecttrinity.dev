The heroes / Tank

Bastion

Tank Resource: Resolve

The wall at the front. Every hit he blocks builds the Resolve he spends on Vengeful Riposte.

Bastion earns Resolve for each hit he blocks and for each of his own that lands, and spends it on Vengeful Riposte.

Play him yourself, or swap to another hero mid-fight and the party AI plays him for you.

Bastion's skills

  • Bash Primary Bastion bashes the enemies in front of him, building Resolve. Three swings chain into one another.
  • Block Secondary Bastion raises his shield and blocks hits from the front until it tires. Raising it just as a hit lands makes his next damaging skill hit harder.
  • Shield Slam Bastion jumps and slams his shield down on the enemies in front of him, and the blow refills his shield. He takes less damage on the way down.
  • Spear of the Wall Bastion strikes, sends a wave out in front of him, then throws his spear. Its hits pin enemies in place for a moment, though never a boss.
  • Challenger's Roar Bastion roars, dealing damage to every enemy around him and taunting them.
  • Clear Ground Bastion strikes every enemy around him with his shield's stored damage, and clears the lingering pools enemies have left on the ground near him.
  • Vengeful Riposte Bastion spends Resolve on three heavy counterstrikes. Every hit lowers the target's Defense, stacking up to three times.

Tank in each fight

  • Patchwork Step out of the Tank Buster's cone; your shield won't stop it.
  • The Giant Keep him facing you. Swipe and Cleave give no warning.
  • Old Hunger Hold him away from the bile. As Bastion, Clear Ground wipes the pools.
  • Maera Hold her inside The Small Light as it moves.
