Privacy

Project Trinity Studios runs this site. It sets no cookies and runs no trackers, ads or analytics scripts. Nothing you send is sold or shared beyond the services named on this page, and each of them is used only to do the one job given here.

Reading the site

  • The pages are hosted by GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. Like any web server, they see your IP address and browser for each request, to deliver the page and keep the site safe. The studio sees only Cloudflare's totals (how many visits, which pages, which countries), never who you are.
  • The fonts are on this site, so no font service sees you.
  • Your Day or Night pick is kept in your own browser's storage, not in a cookie, and never leaves your device.
  • The gameplay video stays a picture until you press play. Then YouTube's privacy-enhanced player opens, and from that moment YouTube's own privacy terms apply to it.

The forms

Every form is checked by Cloudflare Turnstile, which tells a person from a bot. It reads your IP address and some signals from your browser for that check; the IP address isn't stored with what you send. The forms then go to the studio's small server on Cloudflare, which keeps what is listed below in a database on the studio's Cloudflare account and mails a copy to the project mailbox (Gmail).

The email list

  • What: your email address. Kit, the service that sends the list's mail, keeps it with the date you joined and whether you confirmed, and, like most mail services, may note whether a mail was opened or a link in it was clicked.
  • Why: a note when there's a new devlog post or a new build. Not often.
  • How long: until you leave. Every mail has an unsubscribe link, and pressing it takes you off the list at once.

Requesting a build

  • What: your email address, the platform you picked, the note if you wrote one, whether you asked to join the list, and when you sent it. If a build is sent, which key link went to you is kept too. The mail with your key is sent through Resend.
  • Why: to send you a build, to send each address one key only, and to send your own link again if you ask a second time.
  • How long: while Project Trinity: Shatterbound is in testing, then deleted. Sooner if you ask.

Sending a message

  • What: your email address, your name if you give one, what you write, and any of the page's questions you answer: what kind of note it is, whether the build ran, what you played, how bad a bug was, and the game's report code if you paste one. A report code says which build and fight it came from and what was on screen; it holds nothing about you.
  • Why: to read it, fix what it found, and write back.
  • How long: while Project Trinity: Shatterbound is in testing, then deleted. Sooner if you ask.

Having it removed

Write to hello@projecttrinity.dev from the address you used and say what you'd like removed. Your build request, your messages, the mails about them in the project mailbox and your place on the list are deleted within a month, usually much sooner, and you'll get a reply when it's done. You can also ask to see what is kept about you.

Age

The forms and the list are for people 13 or older.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it. A change to what is kept is said in the next devlog post.
